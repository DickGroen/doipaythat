What quotes often leave unclear

What quotes often leave unclear

Cost breakdown
Whether a quote is a fixed price or an estimate, and what's included in materials versus labour, is not always spelled out clearly.
Extra charges and exclusions
Call-out charges, VAT, and costs for anything found once work begins are sometimes left vague. Worth understanding before you agree.
Terms and timing
Payment terms, timelines and what happens if the job takes longer than expected can affect your options. Understanding them before you sign is generally easier than after.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I speak to someone by phone?

We don't offer phone consultations. Everything is delivered in writing by email, which lets us review every document carefully and keep the service affordable. You'll get something you can read, save, and refer back to.

What happens to my document after I upload it?

It's used only to prepare your overview. It is not shared with third parties, advertisers, or any other organisation.

What types of quotes can I upload?

Tradesperson quotes, contractor estimates, home improvement proposals, repair or renovation quotes, and other service proposals where the cost breakdown, materials, labour or terms are unclear.

Is a quote legally binding once I've received it?

Generally not until you accept it — a quote is normally an offer, and the agreement is formed once you agree to it, often in writing. What counts as acceptance can depend on how the quote is worded, which a review can help clarify.

Can the final price differ from the quote?

It can, particularly with estimates rather than fixed-price quotes, or where the wording allows for additional charges once work begins. Understanding which type you have, and what it allows for, is worth doing before you agree.

What happens after I upload?

You receive a free first overview by email — typically by the next working day before 4pm. This gives you a plain-language summary of what the quote includes and flags anything worth clarifying before you agree. A further overview with suggested wording is available for £29 if you want more.

Is this legal advice?

No. DoIPayThat provides plain-language quote overviews and response guidance. It is not legal advice and does not provide legal representation. For free, regulated advice, Citizens Advice and StepChange are independent organisations that can help.
