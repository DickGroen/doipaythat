What charges often leave unclear

What housing charges often leave unclear

Management fees
What a management fee covers and how it is calculated should be clear from the demand. A total without a breakdown can be difficult to follow.
Maintenance and repair costs
Shared maintenance charges should explain what work was carried out and how the cost has been divided. Items without an explanation are worth asking about before paying.
Shared building costs
How building expenses are allocated between residents should be clear from the demand. The basis of calculation is not always stated.

Frequently asked questions

Things people usually ask

Can I speak to someone by phone? ⌄
We don't offer phone consultations. Everything is delivered in writing by email, which lets us review every document carefully and keep the service affordable. You'll get something you can read, save, and refer back to.
What happens to my document after I upload it? ⌄
It's used only to prepare your overview. It is not shared with third parties, advertisers, or any other organisation.
What types of housing charge can I upload? ⌄
You can upload service charge demands, management fee notices, maintenance invoices, leasehold cost summaries and similar housing-related charges where you want to understand what you are being asked to pay before deciding what to do.
Am I entitled to a breakdown of a service charge? ⌄
In many cases, yes. Leaseholders and tenants receiving service charges are often entitled to request a summary of costs or supporting information. Whether that applies in your situation depends on your agreement. An overview can help clarify what questions are reasonable to raise.
What happens after I upload? ⌄
You receive a plain-language overview by email — typically by the next working day before 4pm. This gives you an overview of the bill and flags any areas worth looking at. A fuller overview with suggested response wording is available for £29 if you want more detail.
Do I have to pay a service charge before I can ask questions? ⌄
In many cases, yes. You can ask written questions about specific charges before the payment deadline. Having a clear, professional question to send makes that easier to do.
Is this legal advice? ⌄
No. DoIPayThat provides plain-language charge reviews and suggested response wording. It does not provide legal advice or legal representation. For complex leasehold matters, specialist advice may be worth seeking.
