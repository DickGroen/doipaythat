What we check

Common billing issues

Estimated vs actual readings
If a bill is based on an estimated reading, you may have been charged for more usage than you actually consumed. Actual reading reconciliation should appear clearly.
Tariffs, standing charges and adjustments
Changes in tariff rate, standing charges or billing adjustments should be clearly identified. Unexplained changes can be worth querying before paying.
Duplicate charges or exit fees
Duplicate billing periods, early exit fees or unclear added charges are sometimes present without clear explanation of how they were calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Things people usually ask

What types of bill can I upload? ⌄
You can upload energy bills (gas and electricity), water bills, broadband or phone bills, service charges, repair invoices and most other billing documents where you want to understand the charges before deciding what to do.
Can a company charge me based on an estimated reading? ⌄
Yes — energy and water providers sometimes bill based on estimates when meter access isn't available. If the estimate seems significantly higher than your actual usage, you are generally entitled to submit an actual reading and have the bill recalculated before paying.
What happens after I upload? ⌄
You receive a free first check by email — typically by the next working day before 4pm. This gives you an overview of the bill and flags any areas worth looking at. A full analysis with a dispute letter is then available for £29 if you want more detail.
Do I have to pay before I query a charge? ⌄
For most service bills, you can raise a query before the payment deadline. Sending a written question about specific charges is a reasonable step — and having a clear, professional letter to send makes that easier.
Is this legal advice? ⌄
No. DoIPayThat provides informational bill reviews and dispute drafts in plain English. It does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
What happens to my document? ⌄
Your document is processed securely for the purpose of the review only. It is not shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.
