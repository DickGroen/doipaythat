FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel a subscription that has already auto-renewed?

It depends on the agreement and timing. In some cases cancellation is still possible after a renewal has started, though what applies varies. Understanding the renewal terms first gives you a clearer basis for any next step.

What if I cancelled but am still being charged?

If you have a record of cancelling but charges have continued, it is worth checking whether the notice period and cancellation method matched what the agreement required. A clear written follow-up is usually the most practical step.

What types of subscriptions can I upload?

Gym memberships, broadband contracts, software subscriptions, streaming services, coaching contracts and other recurring agreements where the terms are not fully clear.
