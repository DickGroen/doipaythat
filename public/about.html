About Us

Helping people understand what they're being asked to pay

DoIPayThat provides independent, plain-language explanations of debt collection and demand letters — so you can understand a document before you decide what to do.

Why I built DoIPayThat

Hi, I'm Dick Groen.

After many years teaching Economics and Law, I kept coming back to a simple question: why are so many important letters, contracts and bills so difficult for ordinary people to understand?

Over the years I saw that many people pay, sign or ignore documents simply because they are unsure what they mean. That led me to build DoIPayThat.

My goal is straightforward: help people understand documents before they decide what to do next.

DoIPayThat does not replace a lawyer, accountant or other professional. Instead, it provides a clear, structured explanation to help you understand what you have received and what questions you may want to ask next. Every document is analysed using carefully designed AI workflows that focus on clarity, consistency and plain language, and the service is continuously improved based on real-world feedback.

I hope it helps you make a more confident decision.

What we do

Many people receive a letter demanding payment and feel uncertain about what it means, whether it's correct, or what their options are. DoIPayThat helps with exactly that moment. You upload the document, and we provide a clear, plain-English overview of what it appears to be asking — followed, if you choose, by a more detailed independent review and a response draft you can use.

Our first overview is free. A fuller analysis is available as a one-time service. There is no subscription and no hidden cost.

What we do not do

Being clear about our limits is part of being trustworthy. DoIPayThat does not:

  • provide legal advice or legal representation;
  • provide financial or debt advice regulated by the FCA;
  • contact creditors, collectors, or credit bureaus on your behalf;
  • make decisions for you, or tell you whether to pay;
  • guarantee any particular outcome.

We provide independent document explanations and educational information. The decision about what to do always remains yours. For regulated advice, we point people to free services such as Citizens Advice and StepChange.

How it works

We use AI-assisted document analysis to read and explain documents in plain language, combined with carefully designed review processes. The technology helps us explain things clearly and affordably — but the service is an informational document review, not an automated decision-maker.
