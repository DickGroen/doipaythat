We help people understand important payment-related documents before they decide what to do.

DoIPayThat is an independent document review service. We explain debt letters, payment demands, bills and similar documents in plain English. Our role is to help you understand what a document says — not to make decisions for you.

  • A first overview is free and does not require card details.
  • A fuller review is optional and costs £49 one time if you choose it.
  • We do not contact creditors, collection agencies or any third party on your behalf.
  • We are not a law firm and do not provide legal representation.
  • Your document is used only to prepare your overview or review.

What we do

We review the document you upload and prepare a written explanation in plain English. Depending on the document, this may include who is contacting you, what amount is being claimed, what added charges appear, what information is missing, and which points may be worth clarifying before you decide what to do.

How the process works

1. Upload your document. You can upload a PDF, JPG or PNG. A phone photo is fine if the text is readable.

2. Receive a free first overview. We send a plain-language overview, usually by the next working day before 4pm.

3. Optional fuller review. If you want more detail or a response draft, you can choose the fuller review for £49. This is optional and only happens if you choose it.

Privacy and document handling

Uploaded documents are used only to prepare the requested overview or review. They are not shared with the company chasing payment, advertisers or unrelated third parties. More detail is available in our Privacy Policy.
