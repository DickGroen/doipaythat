Frequently asked questions

Things people usually ask

Is this legal advice? ⌄
No. DoIPayThat provides informational document analysis and draft response letters. It does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
Can you guarantee the charge will be cancelled? ⌄
No. The review helps identify points that may require clarification or may support an appeal. It does not guarantee cancellation or success.
Do I have to buy the full analysis? ⌄
No. The free first review is free. The full review is optional and includes a more detailed assessment and a draft response letter.
Does this work for private parking and council PCNs? ⌄
Yes, but they follow different rules and appeal routes. The review looks at the document type and explains the relevant next steps in plain English.
