Before you upload, here's what most people want to know.

Before uploading a document, most people want to know exactly what happens next — and whether there is any obligation to pay. There isn't.

Here's what happens when you upload:

  • Your document is reviewed individually and explained in plain English.
  • You receive a clear overview of what the letter actually means.
  • That overview is free. No card required. No automatic charge.
  • If you want a fuller review or a response draft, that's a separate, optional step.
  • Your document is deleted once your review is complete.
  • Uploading a document does not create any payment obligation.
