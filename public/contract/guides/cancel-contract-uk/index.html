How to cancel a contract in the UK

Your right to cancel a contract depends on how it was agreed, how long you've had it, and what the contract itself says. In many cases you have more options than the company lets on.

5 min read

The 14-day cooling-off right

If you entered a contract at a distance (online, by phone, or by post) or away from business premises, the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you 14 calendar days to cancel without reason and without penalty.

After the cooling-off period

After 14 days, your right to cancel depends on the contract terms, whether the service has been performed correctly, and whether any cancellation fee is proportionate.

Auto-renewal rules changed in 2022Under new rules for consumer contracts entered after 1 October 2022, fixed-term contracts that auto-renew can be cancelled with one month's notice once the initial term ends.

How to cancel correctly

  • Check the contract for the required notice period and method
  • Give notice in writing and keep proof
  • Quote the contract reference or account number
  • State the date from which you want cancellation to take effect
  • Request written confirmation of the cancellation
