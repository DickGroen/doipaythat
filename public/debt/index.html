Questions

Everything you need to know

When is a debt statute barred?
In England and Wales, most debts become statute barred 6 years after the last payment or written acknowledgement (Limitation Act 1980). Once statute barred, the creditor cannot obtain a County Court Judgment, though the debt technically still exists.
What is a notice of assignment?
When a debt is sold to a third party, the debtor must be notified in writing (s.136 Law of Property Act 1925). Without valid notice, you may not be legally required to pay the new owner.
What if the debt turns out to be valid?
You still receive a full report with a clear explanation and your options — including negotiating repayment. You never pay for an empty report.
Is this legal advice?
No. This is an informational analysis and draft dispute letter. We do not provide legal advice and do not represent you. For complex situations, contact Citizens Advice or a solicitor.
What happens to my document?
